# Privacy & Data Protection Policy

**Credixo Microfinance Platform**
Version 1.1 · Effective Date: 16 August 2026

---

## 1. Overview

This policy describes how Credixo collects, processes, retains, and erases personal and financial data submitted through the Credixo digital lending platform, in compliance with:

- **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023** and **DPDP Rules 2025**
- **RBI Digital Lending Directions 2026**
- **Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA) 2002** and its Rules
- **Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices) Rules 2011**

---

## 2. Data We Collect

| Category | Fields | Source | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity | Full name, gender, age, marital status, education | Applicant self-declaration | KYC & credit underwriting |

## 3. Consent

### 3.1 DPDP Consent (Purpose-Limited)

At intake, applicants provide explicit, granular consent under the DPDP Act 2023. Each consent record captures:

- **dpdp_consent_id** — UUID stored alongside the application record
- **Purpose** — "Credit Underwriting & KYC Verification" (not used for marketing)
- **Timestamp** — ISO 8601 UTC
- **Withdrawal path** — Applicants may withdraw via grievance.officer@credixo.in

### 3.2 AA / ULI Data Pull Consent

A separate opt-in consent is collected for Account Aggregator (Sahamati-registered AA) and ULI data linkage. Declining does not bar access to credit assessment — it results in all affected fields being scored as self_reported with a 0.75x weight adjustment.

## 4. Data Retention Policy

Statutory minimum retention under PMLA and RBI Directions is **5 years** from the date of account closure or loan repayment.

| Data Category | Retention Period | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Loan application & credit assessment records | 5 years from closure | PMLA 2002, RBI Digital Lending Directions 2026 |
| KYC documents | 5 years from account closure | PMLA Rule 9 |
| DPDP consent records | Duration of processing + 5 years | DPDP Rules 2025, Rule 7 |
| Audit logs (AI decisions, SHAP explanations, officer overrides) | 5 years | RBI IT Framework 2021 |
| Behavioral proxy data (electricity, mobile recharge) | 3 years | DPDP Act Section 8(7) |

---

## 5. Erasure & Right to Be Forgotten

Under **DPDP Act 2023, Section 12(c)**, data principals have the right to request erasure after the statutory retention period has elapsed.

**Erasure Process:**
1. Submit erasure request to grievance.officer@credixo.in with subject: DATA ERASURE REQUEST - [Application ID]
2. GRO verifies statutory hold expiry within 7 working days
3. If no legal hold applies, data is pseudonymized and identifiers purged within 30 days
4. Erasure certificate issued to the applicant

Data subject to active PMLA holds or pending litigation cannot be erased until the legal obligation lapses.

## 7. AI & Automated Decision-Making

All loan decisions generated by the Credixo AI system:
- Are **not solely automated** — all "Human Review" decisions involve a loan officer
- Are accompanied by **human-readable SHAP explanations** (top reasons surfaced in the Result page)
- May be **appealed** within 90 days of the decision date

Rejected applicants may formally contest a decision within **90 days**:

3. GRO reviews alongside original SHAP explanations and officer notes
4. Resolution within **30 days** (aligned with GRO SLA)

- Audit logs: immutable, append-only
